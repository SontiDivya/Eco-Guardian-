About

A community looking after its own backyard

Smart Village Eco-Guardian is a web-based project that encourages villagers to protect the environment and maintain a clean and healthy village. Anyone can learn a tip, report a problem and watch the neighbourhood improve.

📣

Report in seconds

Spotted garbage, dirty roads or a water problem? Send a report and the village can act on it quickly.

🌍

Everyday eco tips

Small daily habits — sorting waste, saving water, planting trees — add up to a big difference for everyone.

🤝

Powered by neighbours

The platform works best when the whole village takes part, one street and one household at a time.
